Liability waiver

Everyone who shoots here signs this — members, guests and visitors. Signing at home saves ten minutes in the rain.

Acknowledgement of risk

Shooting sports involve the use of firearms and live ammunition. Risks include, but are not limited to: permanent hearing damage, eye injury, lacerations, burns from hot brass and gases, ricochet, lead exposure, falls on uneven and wet ground, and serious injury or death caused by the negligence of yourself or others present. Weather, terrain and wildlife in Southeast Alaska add further risk. These risks cannot be eliminated.

Your agreement

By signing you confirm that you have read and will follow the club's range rules; that you will obey a cease fire called by any person; that you are legally permitted to possess firearms and ammunition; that you are not under the influence of alcohol or any impairing substance; and that you assume the risks described above.

You further agree to release and hold harmless the Ketchikan Rod & Gun Club, its officers, directors, members and volunteers from claims arising from your participation, except for claims arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct, to the extent permitted by Alaska law.

Sign

The club keeps the signed record on file. Minors under 18 must have this signed by a parent or guardian.
