Eye and ear protection for outdoor ranges

Hearing damage is cumulative and permanent. Eye hits are instant. Both are optional injuries — dress for the line every time, including when you are “just watching.”

2026-08-06 · 5 min read · Ketchikan Rod & Gun Club

Eyes

Wear impact-rated glasses (look for ANSI Z87.1). Tinted lenses help on bright water days; clear lenses are better under heavy overcast and in the timber shadow on the KRGC line. Keep a microfiber cloth handy — Southeast rain turns lenses into impressionist art.

Ears

  • Muffs — easy on/off, good for coaches and spectators.
  • Foam plugs — high NRR if inserted correctly; combine with muffs for centerfire sessions.
  • Electronic muffs — useful for match commands; still bring plugs as backup when batteries die.

Spectators and kids

If they are near the line, they wear eyes and ears too. Spare muffs usually live in the shack — ask. Do not let curiosity cost someone their hearing.

KRGC rule: eyes and ears on the line. Read the range rules, then show up prepared on your first visit.
