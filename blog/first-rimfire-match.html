Your first rimfire match: what to expect at KRGC

The rimfire match is the easiest door into competition at KRGC. Show up with a .22, or empty-handed on loaner days, and you will leave knowing more than you did at 10:00.

2026-08-03 · 8 min read · Ketchikan Rod & Gun Club

When and where

Rimfire is scheduled on the first Saturday of the month at 10:00 — confirm the next date on the matches section or the home-page next-match tile. The range is eleven miles north of downtown on North Tongass Highway. Read hours, rules, waiver, and directions before you drive.

What to bring

  • Signed liability waiver
  • Eye and ear protection
  • Any .22 LR rifle (irons or glass)
  • About 60 rounds, plus a few extras
  • Rain gear you can shoot in

No rifle? Ask about loaners — they often go out to first-timers on match day.

How the day runs

  1. Check in at the shack. Say if it is your first match.
  2. Safety brief — cease fire, muzzle, and stage flow.
  3. Five stages, field positions and barricades, steel and paper.
  4. Scoring feeds the Rimfire Cup standings.
  5. Tear down and talk — the best coaching often happens while casing rifles.

New shooter rule. Tell the range officer you are new. Nobody here started out knowing the choreography. They will put you in the right squad.

Mindset

You are not there to win the cup on day one. You are there to run a safe rifle under a clock and learn what your zero does under rain. Shoot clean. Ask questions. Come back next month.
