    Non-negotiable

    Range rules

    These are the whole membership agreement, minus the paperwork.

    1. Treat every firearm as loadedIncluding the one you just checked.
    2. Muzzle downrange, alwaysNever cover anything you aren't willing to destroy.
    3. Finger off the triggerUntil the sights are on target and you've decided to fire.
    4. Know what's beyondShots stay in the berm. Can't see the impact area? Don't send it.
    5. Actions open when anyone is downrangeChamber flag in, magazine out, hands off the bench.
    6. Eyes and ears on the lineSpectators too. Spare muffs are in the shack.
    7. No alcoholBefore or during. Save it for after the guns are cased.
    8. Daylight onlyYou must be able to see your backstop.
    Cease fire

    Anyone may call it, at any time, for any reason, and nobody has to justify it afterward. On the call: stop, unload, open the action, bench the firearm, step back. The line goes hot again only when the person who called it says so.

    On the calendar

    Matches

    Casual competition, year round, weather included. Never shot a match? Start with the rimfire one.

    First Saturday · 10:00

    Rimfire match

    Any .22 LR rifle, irons or glass. Steel from field positions and barricades, five stages, about three hours, roughly 60 rounds. Loaner rifles and ammunition on hand for anyone who turns up empty.

    New shooters welcome
    Third Saturday · 11:00

    Metallic silhouette

    Chickens, pigs, turkeys and rams from 40 to 100 metres. Rifle and pistol classes shoot the same course. Knock it down or it doesn't count.

    Rifle & pistol
    Sundays, summer

    Trap night

    Five stations, clays and a club shotgun if you don't have one. Runs once the light is long enough.

    Seasonal
    Spring & fall

    Work party

    Berm repair, brush cutting, target frames, roof work. The range exists because members show up to these.

    Members

    Match reminders

    One message the week before each match — what it is, what to bring, whether the weather is going to make it interesting. No other mail, ever.
