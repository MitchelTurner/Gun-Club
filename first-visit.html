Your first visit

Cell service drops out before the gate. Read this before you leave town — the whole walkthrough works offline once you have loaded the site.

  1. Sign the waiver at homeDo it on waiver.html before you drive. Printing a copy helps if the shack Wi‑Fi is down.
  2. Drive north on TongassAbout eleven miles from downtown. Look for the club turnoff on the left heading north. Confirm mile marker and signage.
  3. Park and walk to the shackDo not walk downrange. Find a member or the range officer before you uncase anything.
  4. Eyes and ears onSpare muffs are in the shack. Spectators wear them too.
  5. Learn the line commands“Cease fire” means stop now — anyone can call it. Actions open when people are downrange.
  6. Ask before you send itIf you are new to a bay, say so. Someone will put you on the right line.

Full rules live on the home page safety section. The range diagram is under The range. Match times are under Matches.
