Step 02

Range rules

Learn these before you uncase. Full list lives on the home page.

  1. Treat every firearm as loadedIncluding the one you just checked.
  2. Muzzle downrange, alwaysNever cover anything you aren't willing to destroy.
  3. Finger off the triggerUntil the sights are on target and you've decided to fire.
  4. Know what's beyondShots stay in the berm. Can't see the impact area? Don't send it.
  5. Actions open when anyone is downrangeChamber flag in, magazine out, hands off the bench.
  6. Eyes and ears on the lineSpectators too. Spare muffs are in the shack.
  7. No alcoholBefore or during. Save it for after the guns are cased.
  8. Daylight onlyYou must be able to see your backstop.
Cease fire

Anyone may call it, at any time, for any reason. On the call: stop, unload, open the action, bench the firearm, step back. The line goes hot again only when the person who called it says so.

Step 03

Sign the waiver

Everyone who shoots here signs — members, guests, and visitors. Do it at home; printing a copy helps if the shack Wi‑Fi is down.

Liability waiver

Takes a few minutes. Required before you shoot. Bring a signed printout or confirmation if you can.

Step 04

Getting there

Map sketch and turn-by-turn stay on this page so you can use them with no signal.

  1. Leave downtown northboundTake North Tongass Highway toward Ward Cove / the north end.
  2. Pass mile 11Watch for the club sign near mile 11. The turnoff is easy to miss in the rain.
  3. Park at the lotDo not walk downrange. Find a member or the range officer before you uncase anything.
  4. Eyes and ears onThen ask which bay is open and what the line is doing today.
